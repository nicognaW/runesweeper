# Hints teach reasoning; they never show probabilities

The hint exists to teach players to play like top players, not to play for them. It never shows a mine probability or a solver's verdict, and never names a cell unless the reason can be checked by hand on the board: a deduction, a named pattern, a small count of arrangements, or a guessing principle strong players use. The exact solver still runs underneath, but only to choose which reason to give and which cell to point at. When nothing a player could follow favours any cell, the hint says a guess is needed and names no cell.

## Considered Options

- Showing the safest cell with its percentage, as JSMinesweeper does: rejected because it answers the question without teaching how to reach the answer.
